## Purpose

This model predicts whether an online shopping session will end in a purchase, enabling targeted interventions to increase conversion rates. It supports marketing strategies by classifying sessions into buyer, hesitant, or unlikely categories based on purchase probability.

### Intended uses

- Targeting hesitant returning visitors with coupons to encourage purchases.
- Inviting hesitant new visitors to chat for assistance during their session.
- Segmenting online shopping sessions to optimize marketing interventions.
- Analyzing session-level purchase likelihood for business insights.

### Out of scope

- Real-time prediction during ongoing sessions, as data are from completed sessions only.
- Using the model with features that leak the outcome, such as PageValues.
- Predicting exact purchase amounts or revenue beyond binary purchase classification.
- Applying the model to different online stores without retraining or validation.

## Training data

The model was trained on the UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention dataset, comprising 12,330 sessions from one online store over one year. Each session represents a unique user and is complete, simulating a full visit. The data were split into training (8,630 sessions), validation (1,850 sessions), and test (1,850 sessions) sets, each with a 15.5% purchase rate, stratified on the target. ## Limitations

- The data consist of completed sessions, so the model simulates visit outcomes rather than providing real-time predictions.
- The model's recall at the fixed 50% threshold is low (0.122), indicating many buyers are not identified as such.
- The model does not incorporate features that leak the outcome, such as PageValues, limiting maximum achievable performance.
- The dataset is from a single online store and may not generalize to other domains or stores without retraining.
- Fixed thresholds for buyer, hesitant, and unlikely segments were set in advance and not tuned, which may affect optimal segmentation.
- The model's performance is limited by the inherent class imbalance with only 15.5% purchase rate.

## Ethical considerations

- Coupons are offered only to hesitant returning visitors, who represent 24.3% of returning sessions, potentially favoring this group.
- Chat invitations are extended to hesitant new visitors, comprising 46.8% of new visitor sessions, ensuring support for this group.
- No interventions are given to unlikely segments and some hesitant other visitors, which may affect user experience.
- The model's segmentation may lead to differential treatment based on visitor type, requiring monitoring for fairness.
- Use of the model should consider privacy and transparency regarding targeted marketing interventions.
- The comparison model using PageValues is excluded from deployment due to outcome leakage, avoiding misleading performance claims.
